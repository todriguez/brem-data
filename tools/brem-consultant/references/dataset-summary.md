## Cross-Validation: Central Bank Settlement Pilots (Scored March 2026)

These three projects were scored independently using BREM + extensions. All have near-identical Law domains (~1.33) and similar institutional backing. The entire risk differential is architectural — driven by platform dependency count.

| Project | N (platforms) | Per-fn N | sm_eff | Network | System | Law | Overall | Key Pattern |
|---------|--------------|----------|--------|---------|--------|-----|---------|-------------|
| Fnality International | 1 (Autonity) | 1 | 3 | 2.33 | 2.33 | 1.33 | **2.00** | Single-platform, BoE settlement finality. Passes all thresholds. |
| Project Guardian (MAS) | 7+ | 2-3 | 3-4 | 3.00 | 2.67 | 1.33 | **2.33** | Modular multi-platform: per-use-case isolation. Borderline domain ceiling. |
| Project Acacia (RBA) | 5 | 5 | 4 | 3.00 | 3.00 | 1.33 | **2.44** | Systemic multi-platform: all must interoperate. At domain ceiling boundary. |

**Key finding:** Dependency decomposition penalty should scale with per-function N (platforms that must interoperate for a single settlement function), not total platform count. Guardian has MORE total platforms than Acacia but LOWER risk because each function only touches 2-3 platforms.
